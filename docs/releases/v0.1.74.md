# v0.1.74: reviewed concurrent candidate refresh

Two authors can prepare candidates from one accepted revision, publish one, then
explicitly refresh the other while preserving its reviewed intent. Refresh checks
complete authored dependency footprints, including negative lookups and empty
relation ranges, and renews semantic validation, impact and selected tests against
one exact current target. Created identities and authored after-values remain fixed.

Retain the original request and its ordinary review token. After another candidate
publishes, observe current status and prepare a renewed review:

```text
lkjscript --project app status
lkjscript --project app change refresh --input-file original.lkjc \
  --plan ORIGINAL_TOKEN --onto CURRENT_REVISION --output refreshed.logical-plan
```

Inspect the renewed candidate, then explicitly apply the unchanged original input:

```text
lkjscript --project app change apply --input-file original.lkjc --plan REFRESHED_TOKEN
```

The original request keeps its original base. A `refresh_` token binds the request,
original prepared candidate, exact onto revision and renewed prepared candidate.
A conflicting read or write rejects refresh; a writer that advances HEAD before
apply makes the unaccepted publication stale. Refresh neither retries publication
automatically nor chains refreshed origins. Exact accepted keyed retries return
their immutable result after later descendants; another target under the same key
conflicts.

The fixed intent footprint admits at most 1,000,000 guards and 64 MiB of guard
bytes. Authenticated single-parent ancestry admits at most 10,000 revisions under
one bounded canonical-read meter. Original reconstruction, guard rechecking and
renewed preparation share request read/work allowance. Cancellation, incomplete
footprints, exhausted admission and corrupt or disconnected history fail closed.

Supplied type closure remains complete even when storage already contains equal
bytes; physical deduplication no longer changes reviewed intent. CLI observation
37 reports `summary supplied-types` and refresh provenance. Transaction 6, semantic
diff 4, receipt 6 and logical plan 6 bind the updated evidence. Graph and compiler
generations remain unchanged; ordinary runtime artifacts retain their existing
format. Accepted-history predecessor envelopes remain readable under their original
encoding; logical review files must be regenerated through the current owner.
Replanning an older accepted request cannot reproduce its predecessor transaction identity;
its occupied idempotency key remains immutable, while exact current-transaction retries remain supported.

Finalized candidate acceptance contract 3 requires every enumerated `native_refresh`
public-harness case alongside existing native families. Historical accepted
terminals retain their original obligations. Source, finalized-byte and public
acceptance are pending and remain separate facts in [current status](../status.md).
See the [semantic contract](../spec/concurrent-changes.md) and
[verification obligations](../spec/verification.md#reviewed-concurrent-change-obligations).
