# 0.1.96

Source-tied associated read-view families let a consumer select a supplier's
scoped nominal descriptor while preserving private representation and field
authority. Exact contract, member, witness and owner-qualified scope inventories
survive generic substitution, package transport and independent artifact admission.
The range witness composes Flat and private Chunked views through a generic
transformer.

Views are affine handles to immutable descriptor backing. Explicit construction
and reborrow retain exact source lineage and ancestor custody; subviews may retain
backing after a local parent handle is dropped without widening the source scope.
Passive custody grants no read access. The single callable result contract
distinguishes `Value`, `ReadFrom(source)` and `ViewFrom(source, scope)`. Scoped
joined reads preserve those rights and cleanup obligations; capture, transfer and
escape do not acquire permission to retain views.

The explicit compatibility cut selects Graph34, Interface22 and
compiler34/bytecode28/artifact41, with Summary12/Witness10/Validator41. Closed
ordinary type objects remain TypeObject10; view forms use the dedicated capability1
envelope. TypeObject11 is unsupported. Bounded predecessor readers preserve
original profiles and proof identities before complete current admission; old
evidence does not attest the new semantics. The [specification](../spec/associated-read-views.md)
records the complete encoding inventory and refusal boundaries.

Candidate acceptance and workload generation 12 require all four exact public
read-view cases and the complete read-view namespace, including detached results,
source/category refusals, joined reads, passive custody and trap/fuel cleanup.
