This identity selects ordinary data parameters in owned contracts and generic
implementation schemes. [Current status](../status.md) owns actual source
acceptance, mainline delivery and publication.

Transferable or CaptureSafeTransferable; implementation schemes retain their
stronger Owned bounds and add the two ordinary bounds. Complete admission checks
ordinary category independently from transfer/capture obligations, including
unused and phantom arguments. Ordinary parameters are unrestricted, ordinary
results are immutable standalone values, and borrowed results retain their exact
owned source relationship.

the six-method `IndexedData` contract with Flat and private nominal Chunk32 storage.

witnesses across all four flat/chunked combinations, retaining authored outcomes,
capacities, append-only readiness and its independent complete-result checker.
