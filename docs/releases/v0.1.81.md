# v0.1.81 — Scoped parallel reads and borrowed task composition

Named graph tasks can borrow owned inputs synchronously, including exact task
methods whose Self argument borrows. These calls require Owned and retain their
normal callable kind, effect allowances and deployment grants. A task borrow
cannot consume, capture, store or return its source as a borrowed task result.

Joined parallel children can read the same owner through a separate structural
Shareable obligation. Native generic parameters spell this
`(constraint owned shareable)` or, when movement is also required,
`(constraint owned transferable shareable)`. Shareable and Transferable are
independent: shared reads preserve the source custodian, while consuming inputs
and owning results require transfer admission. Synchronous borrowing needs no
Shareable assumption. Owned-contract Self retains its existing Owned constraint;
generic implementation schemes preserve any stronger obligations required by
their mapped functions.

Both child argument lists are evaluated once in the parent, in left-then-right
order. Their complete loan footprints remain active through the join. Repeated
reads of one root are valid; read/consume aliases, including protected ancestors,
reject in either order. Nested children delegate only their admitted read rights.
Every started descendant joins and ends its loans before the source guard or
owner can be reclaimed, including cancellation, traps and reservation refusal.
Storage locks do not span user computation. Existing payload allocations and
owner origins remain intact through shared access and subsequent owner reuse.

Generic function and witness applications check their exact declared bounds,
complete method maps and prerequisite identities. The actual values crossing a
child boundary separately require their complete structural transfer or sharing
proof, including inactive cases and phantom arguments inside carried types.
Unrelated application metadata acquires no blanket transfer requirement. Strict
canonical, independent ownership/reference and artifact admission retain these
obligations across unused declarations and untaken syntax.

The [three-package witness](../../examples/scoped-parallel-reads/README.md)
exports recursive range-sum tasks before concrete items exist. The same algorithms
read flat and chunked cells, buffers and nested products through exact Element and
IndexRead witnesses. Ranges of at most 32 items use the sequential indexed helper;
larger ranges split into joined recursive reads of the same storage. Distinct
observers retain their witness identities, and a borrowed task method maps to the
same generic sum. Consumers drain the unchanged original in LIFO order and reuse
its empty owner. Matched serial and parallel observations separate preparation,
invocation and modeled work; runtime speedups and API-cost savings are not
established by storage sharing.

Children remain direct named graph tasks with closed empty effect rows. Pure
callers, borrowed task results, detached lifetimes and manufactured read authority
remain unsupported. Scoped reads grant no mutation, ownership or external-effect
authority. General regions, channels and scheduling policy remain separate work.

The selected identities are graph/owner 30, validator 35, package interface 18,
compiler unit 30, bytecode 25, artifact 37, authored change 34 and compact change
38. Constraint tags 0–5 retain their bytes; tags 6 and 7 encode Owned Shareable
and Owned Transferable Shareable. Rebuild derived bundles and maintained inputs
through their supported product owners before accepting the new source and
finalized executable. Earlier proof does not attest the new semantics or bytes;
original historical fixtures and immutable publication evidence retain their
separate identities.
