# Optimization ledger

On 2026-11-25 the owner replaced the **10% wall-time retention bar** and the
**12% CallSRA census kill switch** with a bar of **about 5% measured wall-time
reduction**, verified by a passing differential of optimized native execution
against the independent interpreter on the original checked lkjscript IR. Use
the fixed workload and output oracle, with a matched baseline and uninstrumented
timings. Recheck transformed IR independently and discard rejected output.
Static eligibility, sampled opportunity and timing proxies can justify a trial.
Retention requires measured wall time and the differential.

Record every optimization approach here when evaluated, including retained,
weak, counterproductive, superseded, reverted, census-cut and reopened approaches.
Preserve the original decision and append later results with their workload and
revision. Positive reductions mean lower wall time. The numbered pass count and
Rust `-O` milestones follow [Direction](direction.md#build-optimizer-passes-then-measure).
