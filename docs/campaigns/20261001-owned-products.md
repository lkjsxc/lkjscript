## Selected scope

Implement explicit structural owned products with fixed named typed fields,
construction in authored evaluation order, whole synchronous owner transfer,
complete consuming lexical decomposition, and deterministic failure cleanup.
Products can carry closed ordinary first-order metadata, ByteBuffer, OwnedI64Cell,
nested finite products and in-scope Owned parameters. At least one direct owned
field is required. Ordinary records and containers retain their
unrestricted contracts and cannot carry owned products. No partial moves, borrowed
field projections, mutable or escaping borrows, asynchronous transfers, task memory
signatures, nominal owned schemes or implicit witness search are selected.

All semantic, authoring, transport, compilation, independent admission and runtime
layers must agree. Product tokens are separately sealed and retain exact invocation
origin and closed type; raw Clone is inert. Construction/decomposition must preserve
child allocation identity and reserve storage before allocation or detachment.
Admission includes unused/phantom arguments, nominal closures and untaken syntax.
Existing finite bounds, ordinary/capture safety and capability authority remain.
Use additive encodings where possible and freeze or refuse changed predecessors.

Evidence requires literal native three-package generic composition, both existing
carriers, nesting and multiple owned children, exact witnesses, independent values,
physical allocation identity, public drafts/edits/transport/detached execution,
hostile canonical and derived controls, cleanup observations in both evaluators,
focused tests and workspace all-target/all-feature Clippy. Full/release acceptance,
review, commit and push belong to the main integrator.
