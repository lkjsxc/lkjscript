## 1. Objective, mode, and stopping rule
This is an **implementation vertical slice** followed by proof, not another planning or measurement
pass.
**Final state:** unreleased source product `0.1.19` uses one crash-safe, rebuildable, incremental
object-location catalog for healthy `GraphRepository` open, lookup, and pack sealing while immutable
packs and accepted `HEAD` remain canonical; a copied release-mode `lkjscript` binary constructs,

The bottleneck is physical catalog work. At the orientation revision, every `PackDirectoryStore` open scans all pack metadata and rebuilds
the complete `ObjectCatalog` before consulting `catalog/current.lkjc`; every seal repeats that work
and rewrites the full catalog.

historical attempt reached 275,000 modules in 3,611 seconds before the one-hour ceiling with memory
and disk still available.

cutover, current owners, coherent commits, and a clean or exactly preserved tree.

## 4. Target contract
### 4.1 Authority and layout
After cutover:
- `HEAD` and its exact immutable object closure remain canonical.
- Pack contract 1, object-store contract 1, and content-addressed object identities remain unchanged.
- Catalog manifests/segments remain derived and excluded from semantic, compiler, artifact, package,
  release, deployment, and runtime identity.
- A catalog hit is usable only after the referenced pack entry reproduces the exact typed key,
  length, checksum, and domain.
- Full footer reconstruction remains recovery and independent verification; its oracle must not
call the contract-2 reader, lookup, or merge implementation.
Replace monolithic catalog contract 1 with deterministic bounded contract 2:
```text
catalog/current.lkjc    atomically replaced current manifest
catalog/segments/       immutable content-addressed sorted segments
```
A segment binds version, level/generation, count, exact key range, canonical ordered locations,
referenced pack descriptors, checksum, and closing marker. The manifest binds
the complete selected segment set, level/order invariants, total entries/packs, aggregate logical
catalog commitment, and exact segment digests.
Lengths, counts, levels, ranges, paths, arithmetic, decoded bytes, and lookup work need typed
limits. Reject or rebuild unknown, duplicate, noncanonical, corrupt, traversing,
symlinked, non-regular, truncated, trailing, overflowing, or exhausted state.
Use deterministic leveled immutable segments with streaming merge, or an observably equivalent
structure: bounded segment lookup; one delta per accepted pack set;
deterministic logarithmic merge work; no healthy full catalog materialization or old-pack footer
scan; and no complete prior-entry rewrite after every batch.
Do not introduce SQLite, a daemon, `unsafe` mapping, checkout-only acceleration, cache authority,
or mutable in-place visibility.

### 4.2 Healthy open, lookup, and seal
On a valid contract-2 repository:
- open reads the manifest and bounded segment metadata, not every old pack footer or catalog entry;
- it proves that manifest-selected locations can reconstruct the exact current `HEAD` closure; it
  need not discover interruption-leftover packs unreachable from `HEAD`;
- lookup/contains searches the bounded live segment set and verifies the selected pack entry;
- seal derives delta locations from newly sealed pack metadata already in memory;
- streaming merges are deterministic and bounded;
- new segments are durable before the manifest references them;
- the manifest is durable before a new `HEAD` may require the new objects;
- old manifest visibility remains valid until atomic replacement;
- obsolete derived segments are removed only after new manifest durability and only by exact owned
  path; interrupted leftovers are bounded, detectable, and cleanable; and
- healthy open/seal observations report zero complete footer scans and zero full rebuilds.
Segment arrangement, merge, regeneration, or cleanup must not rename or change accepted meaning.

### 4.3 Rebuild, migration, and interruption
A missing, contract-1, malformed, current-closure-incomplete, or lookup-inconsistent catalog
triggers at most one bounded full reconstruction from immutable pack footers under correct locking. Successful
reconstruction atomically publishes contract 2 and retries. Canonical pack/object corruption rejects with a stable actionable diagnostic and leaves `HEAD`
unchanged.
Contract 1 is disposable predecessor state, not a parallel supported reader. Delete its healthy
decoder/writer and dual-path tests. Retain one bounded predecessor fixture proving first open rebuilds
from packs into contract 2. Regenerate maintained catalog state through its typed owner without
changing maintained `HEAD` or canonical packs.
Fault injection covers segment durability, manifest staging/visibility, cleanup, pack sealing,
and `HEAD` replacement. Reopen observes the complete old or complete new revision. Catalog
failure after pack durability may require rebuild, but must not report a false failed write after
accepted `HEAD` changed.
