### Success contract

A 304 returns its original ordered headers and an empty body immediately after
complete headers, without reading a representation or applying its metadata
length to the response-body allocation limit. Its canonical decimal metadata
may exceed a machine integer. A valid 204 also ends at headers; prohibited 204
Content-Length (including zero) and Transfer-Encoding reject. Existing duplicate,
ambiguous, malformed and unsupported framing rejection remains. Already buffered
bytes after a bodyless response reject; this does not promise detection of future
packets after the client has completed and closed its non-reused connection.

Every chunk size/extension line is bounded to 8,192 wire bytes including CRLF,
regardless of buffering or fragmentation. Exact-fit lines work; one-over lines
reject before body publication. Ordinary sized, chunked and close-delimited
responses, truncation, body limits, trailers and recovery retain their contracts.
