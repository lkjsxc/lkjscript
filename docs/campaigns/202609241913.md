## Retained boundary and next engineering priority

The known host-process defect remains: direct child exit is not stream completion.
`process::run_inner` can stop deadline enforcement before joining inherited output
pipes; ordinary `run`/`run_selected` do not supervise descendants by default.
Identity probing is another affected path. Merely turning on optional sampling or
killing the original group does not establish cleanup for separately grouped or
unobserved descendants. Do not infer its repair from this accepted PR or guide.

Next host work should keep child status, stream completion and owned-resource
cleanup separate; enforce bounds until the complete operation ends, cancel/join
both readers on every error path, preserve identity when signaling/reaping, and
exercise inherited stdout/stderr, separate groups, timeout, exhaustion and cleanup
failure. Reuse existing process/evidence owners. Do not add another receipt system,
drop reader threads or treat this as a hostile-process sandbox. Revisit mandatory
resident metering separately from live resource/deadline controls with actual
execution tests; this guide neither changes nor endorses it as a permanent rule.
