Previously a private nongeneric resource helper could only consume its final requirement-bound
parameter. This campaign admits an explicit final borrow under the same private, same-package,
direct-call, exact-requirement, acyclic restrictions. It does not introduce general references,
regions, resource results, resource containers, asynchronous borrowing, or cross-instance transfer.
Borrow is non-consuming capability access, not an assertion of purity or immutable memory.

Both semantic validators distinguish a borrowed parameter from an owning local. Preparation binds
the exact canonical parameter-use mode; both execution engines attenuate borrow handles and reject
consumption of a non-owning view. A borrow retains the existing task scope and does not allocate
another resource slot. Ordinary caller ownership remains available after normal return. Errors,
cancellation, and cleanup do not restore consumed rights or replay external queue transitions.
The `affine_capability_resources` validation feature advances from 2 to 3 without an encoding
change. Automatic affine extraction remains consume-only.
