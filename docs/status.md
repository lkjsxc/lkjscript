## Exact compilation impact

[Issue #9](https://github.com/lkjsxc/lkjscript/issues/9) is repaired in this selected
source, not yet delivered. The old planner consulted the candidate record when a
new Port had no before ownership path, incorrectly selecting the Port itself in
addition to its component. Exact allocated IDs reproduce this defect with one or
two ports, with and without a Command target. The compiler's strict corruption
check is unchanged; invalid unit domains are not silently filtered.

The [projection decision](decisions/exact-compiler-unit-projection.md) separates
independent semantic admission from enclosing compilation units. Missing paths
terminate only their own snapshot; moved children retain both parents and deleted
children retain the before path. The semantic/compiler sets share one projection,
while direct port contract checking, cycle detection and resource limits remain.
This reduces one repeated traversal, not a measured whole-application speed ratio.

Focused source evidence: all ten new library cases pass, including complete
incremental manifest/artifact equality with clean builds for creation and accepted
port add/rebind/move/delete/component removal. The isolated ownership matrix makes
11,664 complete comparisons against forward descendant closure; its synthetic
maps are not claims of complete program admission. Exact-fit and under-budget
refusal, both-snapshot cycles and unchanged authority remain checked.

The original baseline contains four genuine Port-ID failures and one mistaken

to assert refusal without authority/cache changes, not counted as a product defect.
