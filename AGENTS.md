- Add an everyday form when it lowers to existing K or ordinary source library.

Use located diagnostics with source types, names and supported rewrites.

normal K checking and sealing of the raw interface. Bounds and arithmetic remain
checked. Views may occupy locals, arguments, results and view aggregates, not
