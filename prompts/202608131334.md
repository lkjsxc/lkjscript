### Payload Binding Semantics

- A named payload binding gets one stable public entity after successful publication.
- Its owner is the exact match arm or callable relation already used by current workspace
  indexing.
- Its declared type is the substituted concrete field type.
- Its lexical visibility begins only inside its arm body.
- It is not visible in the scrutinee, another arm, or an earlier pattern sibling unless
  existing semantics explicitly permit that scope.
- A failed transaction consumes no payload-binding identity.
- A replaced or deleted containing match tombstones the binding according to current
  containment rules.
